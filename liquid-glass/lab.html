What this can tell us.

Observation. Apple's examples support relatively quiet cores and stronger shoulders in some scenes. Regular and Clear are different materials; there is no single recovered Apple lens.

Experiment. Compare a bounded inverse displacement field with a rounded height field and two refracting interfaces. Blur, synthetic reflection and contrast are independent. The lab uses a single captured texture, not live DOM capture.

Limit. This is one surface and one backdrop. It does not validate Android rendering, merged selections, iOS parity or phone frame rates. Without WebGL, the disclosed fallback demonstrates motion only.
