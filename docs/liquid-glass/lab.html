Evidence and limits ↗

The same backdrop is used by every preset. Crossed lines reveal bending along both axes.

Two hypotheses, neither a recovered Apple shader. The boundary field separates edge bending from central magnification.

These controls change sampled colors, not source coordinates. Foreground text remains outside the filter.

Contact lowers optical depth and expands the outline. Release restores both using the same spring state.

Model and limitations

Observation. Apple's examples support relatively low central displacement and stronger edge displacement in some scenes. Regular and Clear are different materials; there is no single recovered Apple lens.

Experiment. Compare a bounded inverse displacement field with a rounded height field and two refracting interfaces. Blur, synthetic reflection and contrast are independent. The background is generated once per selection and reused as a texture. It does not capture page content.

Limit. This is one surface and one backdrop. It does not validate Android rendering, merged selections, iOS parity or phone frame rates. When WebGL is unavailable, software rendering applies the same inverse maps at a lower resolution. Both renderers sample the background; their performance depends on the device.
