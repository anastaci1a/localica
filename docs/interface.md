# Interface contract

Use the supplied `localica-reference` screenshots for structure: Apple Music navigation/player, Musicolet lists/queues/scanner, and Poweramp action customization. This document owns visible behavior and UI defaults; [Product](product.md) owns media/playback semantics and [Structure](structure.md) owns implementation.

## Shared controls and motion

- Floating controls morph into related frosted menus/panels and return on dismissal: creation, More, sorting, filters, queue and lyric-source selectors. Keep one visible surface through the window handoff. Position stops at its destination; momentum deforms width/height, allowing edge bulge without center overshoot. Popups share the Queue’s neutral frosted material, with light/dark tints. Long-press menus and confirmations ease into view; full settings/editor/artwork pages slide and fade. Keep exit content until its motion finishes, blocking further actions. Use rounded Apple Music-style icons and attributed open-source transport artwork, omit unavailable actions, and leave system pickers/permissions native. Settings categories: Interface, Playback and queues, Library and tags, About/licenses; use icons, summaries and chevrons, with search only at the root.
- Corner tokens: small art 8 dp, cards/inputs 16, menus 20, large art 24, panels 28. Compact shelves/chips use pills; transport uses circles. Uniform icons start at 24 dp; a unified icon/label/surface target is at least 48 dp. Clip press feedback to the control's shape. Artwork and timed lyric taps have no ripple.
- Sort/settings choices share a moving clear lens with tap and drag control. Selected media sit beneath a shallow, untinted lens: flat/invisible when inactive, rounded at its perimeter when selected. Adjacent selections merge, including narrow diagonal bridges; unselected holes remain clear. Deselecting reverses the effect; partial groups have no lens or checkmark. Switches and slider thumbs also use glass, with shape deformation instead of position overshoot. Long titles pause, scroll and fade at their edges; reduced motion uses ellipsis.
- Gestures track the finger through slow drags, reversals and interruptions, then settle by distance/velocity. Keep the original owner until release: sliders, lyrics, reordering, carousels and overflow bars must not also swipe the parent, even at an edge. Short-screen scrolling takes precedence. One gesture changes one surface level.
- Default motion: surfaces about 400 ms, menus 300 ms, lyrics 350 ms; offer faster/reduced motion. Programmatic scrolling eases to its measured destination without visible overshoot. Keep focus stable, provide accessible/tap alternatives, and accommodate landscape, large fonts and short screens.
- Browsing/settings stay light, with a pink selected-tab accent and shared neutral gray for secondary text and inactive icons. Interface → Appearance adjusts this gray persistently; reset restores the darker default. Use Inter with compact tracking and a bold mini-player title; Apple’s system font is not bundled. Error/destructive states retain red. Floating pills, mini-player, filter/search bars and circular controls share Liquid Glass: curved refraction, illuminated edges and crisp foregrounds. Presses subtly widen/flatten the surface, rebound on release and brighten the rim; reduced motion retains illumination without deformation. The Queue drawer keeps its denser textured frosted material. Broader themes remain [planned](roadmap.md).
- **Interface → Glass** groups live, persistent sliders for blur (clear through frosted), white tint, shadow strength (including off), shadow spread (tight through broad), and adaptive contrast strength, with a preview/reset. Adaptive white/black tint supports the chosen foreground on floating controls and the navbar; media-selection lenses remain untinted. Navigation selector styles: Adjustable glass (independent blur/white tint), Clear overlay lens (refracts the finished bar and labels, disabling but retaining those controls), and Classic gray pill (no glass deformation). Defaults use light scattering/tint and a very subtle, broad shadow. These settings affect floating glass; the Queue drawer keeps its separate material.

## Navigation and Back

| Input/state | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Tabs | Default Home + Library. Allow unique ordered choices from Home, Library, Songs, Albums, Artists, Album Artists, Folders, Playlists, Queues, Most played; retain at least one. Large icons and readable labels share one animated selection color, responding as navigation starts. One capsule responds immediately to taps and magnetically follows manual page swipes. Dragging it uses magnetic resistance toward tab centers and changes the page only after crossing into the next tab; it does not scrub the pager. The capsule masks the accent color across icons and labels. Each new tapped or dragged focus produces one haptic, respecting system settings. Only the lens deforms; the containing pill stays fixed. |
| Swipe tabs | Follow configured order without wrapping; preserve each tab's route/position and retain completed content/artwork for return visits. Headings travel with their pages, aligned to the search/filter inset; changed detail headings crossfade. Commit visit history only after settling. |
| Tap any tab button | Return to its root and ease to the top, retaining presentation. Library clears its filter and animates the bubbles back to their configured order. Re-selecting adds no history. |
| Detail/search Back | Android and in-menu Back restore the same parent query, first visible item and pixel offset. Never restore into temporary empty results. |
| Back priority | Keyboard/topmost popup or dialog → visible queue/playlist selection → queue overlay, artwork viewer or lyrics as applicable → expanded player → detail/search history → tab visits → exit. |
| Preferences/lifecycle | Tab history on, exit confirmation off; disabling history still permits detail/search Back. Recreation restores state; fresh launch prefers Home, otherwise the first tab. Closing the Activity leaves audio running. |

Tabs are 64 dp high; the mini-player and standalone circular controls are four fifths of that height (51.2 dp), excluding system insets. Mini-player artwork is 32 dp, vertically centered with a 16 dp leading inset. Three aligned floating pills follow the Apple Music reference: full-width mini-player above flexible tabs at left and unlabeled Search/More at right. Search is immediately left of More; More offers Scan music, Settings and Close; About/licenses lives only in Settings. Lists draw behind these controls, with enough content padding to reveal final items. Inside album, artist, Album Artist, playlist and folder details, tabs and utility controls leave; the mini-player moves down and remains available. Library/Playlists have an aligned circular + matching the mini-player's height; it morphs into its frosted creation menu and returns on dismissal, offering Create playlist and Import playlist from file ([import rules](product.md#library-and-playlists)).

## Browsing and search

- Use shared catalog/content/search templates. List/gallery settings inherit by list type with optional collection overrides and reset. Album catalogs and album contents are separate types. Gallery **Items per row** snaps to 1–5; preserve legacy presets until changed. Use smaller text/tighter padding at three or more columns; retain full-width galleries with an overlaid scrollbar.
- Outside queues, pinch outward through List → five columns → four → three → two → one, and inward to reverse. Change one adjacent view per gesture, requiring all fingers to lift before another step. Pinch distance linearly interpolates item geometry toward the adjacent layout; reversing restores the source, including items virtualized out by the destination. Release settles to the source or destination and only then saves the view. Keep the nearest item at its vertical position and preserve selection. View controls remain the accessible alternative; reduced motion changes layout immediately.
- With no filter selected, Library combines collections: default Albums, Album Artists, Playlists; optionally Artists/Folders. Settings → Library and tags → Library filters owns visible filters, resting order and **Unfiltered Library collections**. Holding a filter opens a shortcut there. Empty filter/collection sets are valid; hiding the active filter clears selection. Global search retains all types. Labels use **Album Artist / Album Artists**.
- Covers: circles for Artist/Album Artist, rounded squares for albums/playlists; representative member artwork for collections. Show memory-cached covers immediately; newly decoded covers use a subtle loading sweep and 300 ms reveal, with static missing-cover placeholders and reduced-motion support. Gallery actions are on long-press, without overlaid menu buttons.
- Library root has no top search box: bottom Search opens a global-search page. Selecting a glass filter bubble moves it to the far left; the previous selection returns to its configured position, with neighbors yielding progressively and scrolling following the selected bubble. Tapping the selected filter clears it and restores its position; there is no All button. Keep configured order separate from this temporary arrangement. Start bubble movement on tap, with springy neighbors yielding progressively. Retain the previous complete list until matching saved sort/layout and results are ready; publish those together.
- Album contents omit scoped search and its setting. Other lists can enable scoped quick search in **List View Options**. Search pills and filter bubbles share their height and vertical placement (40 dp visible inside a 48 dp target, 4 dp outer vertical inset; grow together for large text). Both float above a nonlinear list-edge fade with initial content below them. Search morphs the pill into one frosted foreground panel and reverses on close. Collapse only visible vacated header space, restoring its original scroll offset; already-scrolled content stays put. Global/local search are not main tabs.
- Search focuses input and raises the keyboard when its field is ready; a morph defers focus until its final approach. Typing edits one draft; IME submission or opening a result commits it. Each completed search retains query, scope, filters, sort, results position and identity. Back retraces searches in order without reopening the keyboard. Opening results/player preserves the source and popup positions. Mixed-result queues include only displayed song occurrences, in order.
- Distinct Sort and View controls use shared menus. Defaults: catalogs/search title ascending; album songs disc/track; playlists/queues saved order; recent newest; frequent most played; pins manual. Offer applicable title/count/duration/year/date/artist/Album Artist/composer/genre fields. Date added/modified start newest-first; Most played highest-first, all reversible. Rules for dates/counts: [Product](product.md#dates-and-listening-statistics).
- Eye buttons open the reference-based English **List View Options** subpage: presentation, Items per row, extra information, applicable grouping and scoped search, retaining inheritance/overrides. Overflowing lists of at least 40 items get a narrow edge scrollbar and accessible scroll action; show letter/year previews only during dragging under title/year sort.

## Collection details

- Album and artist pages use a full-width artwork header feathered into a color continuation behind the songs. Choose black or white foregrounds from the artwork brightness. Sort and visibility share a glass pill where both are present; empty lists show a quiet centered inset-style icon. Back and collection overflow are glass controls; the top color fade depends on scrolling past the artwork and is absent at the top.
- Album headers show the album/artist and a large Play control using the configured song-tap behavior. List rows use muted track numbers, regular song titles and quiet horizontal ellipses. Row overflow reveals glass only during the press/morph; top-level utility menus retain vertical dots.
- Artist and Album Artist pages use the newest album by tagged year, then library-added date, with stable identity as a tie-breaker; no artwork scraping. Below the header, a horizontal album multiselect filters the sorted song list. No explicit selection means All albums; selecting an album replaces All, and deselecting the last restores All. A combined glass Play/Shuffle pill acts on that displayed list. Shuffle randomizes the new queue’s saved order without enabling global traversal shuffle.

## Selection and reordering

- Hide bulk controls until a song is held; the action panel slides/fades in and out over 220 ms, matching selection deselection. Select it and show the shared action panel above the mini-player, retaining tabs; collection holds instead open actions including Select all songs. Normal collection taps navigate. Selected items have rounded outlines; partially selected collections have dashed outlines, never checkboxes.
- Retain source occurrence identities and selection order across routes, tabs, queries, sorting, rotation and backgrounding. Select-all appends in collection order; reselect appends last. Preserve distinct occurrences for playlists/queues and deduplicate files for editing. Fresh sessions start empty.
- Panel: All, Range, count, actions and explicit close; wrap/scroll within a bounded height. Close, mutable-list Back or successful actions exit. Cancel/failure/empty selection retains the mode; collapsing the panel retains selection. Remove-from-source requires one editable source. No separate selection-basket screen. Playlist chooser marks existing membership accessibly while allowing duplicates.
- In a complete, manual, unfiltered queue/playlist, hold-drag moves the held occurrence or selected occurrences from that source, preserving their source order even when nonconsecutive. Cross-source selections and sorted/filtered views cannot reorder.
- Lift visible selected rows into a shadowed stack above all selection controls/list clipping. The first source-order song is on top, trailing cards slightly offset, with a count badge for multiple items. Track the finger directly; keep a one-row insertion gap, ease surrounding rows, edge-scroll and smoothly unpack on release.
- Hide the action panel throughout dragging/landing. A Liquid Glass foreground X circle remains above the stack. Hover tints it red with one firm haptic; leaving gives a lighter tick, honoring system settings. Release there cancels and restores the panel. Other drops, including an unchanged insertion position, finish selection after successful landing. System interruption or failed persistence restores order, selection and the panel; all landings crossfade into normal rows and preserve playback cursors.

## Player and Queue drawer

The mini-player appears whenever an active queue has a current song, including paused playback. Inset its artwork/title together inside the leading curve; use black filled Previous/Play/Pause/Next icons. Play morphs into Pause; skips use two rounded triangles without an end bar, with a staggered directional nudge. Secondary metadata defaults to Album Artist with Artist fallback; Artist-first is configurable. The body is one rounded tap target; transport remains independent.

Tap/swipe up expands one shared outline containing glass, artwork, text and transport. Glass grows blurrier through expansion; the artwork backing blends in later and becomes opaque near full size. Full-background covers soften at their perimeter while keeping the center sharp, then merge into the color continuation; floating covers keep defined edges. All blends reverse with the gesture.

Metadata/seek/actions/lyrics have 32 dp side insets. Order: artwork → metadata → seek/times → customizable actions → transport → Queue drawer; no volume controls. Album metadata fades in and seek/actions appear late, while transport persists throughout. Normal metadata and controls sit below the cover with balanced space above Queue; lyrics mode lowers them further.

A 72 dp translucent handle appears late in expansion below the safe top inset. During collapse it keeps the same inset from the moving top edge and fades early, avoiding control overlap; its accessible tap target also collapses the player. Main tabs leave downward. Playback-starting song taps expand by default (configurable); add-next/append do not. Swipe down/Back restores underlying browse state. During collapse, artwork and its color continuation resolve together before detaching; no stretched backing cover or dark browse flash.

| Queue element | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Drawer | Bottom-attached, visually separate from player artwork, with one neutral frosted surface throughout expansion: blurred backdrop colors, fine texture and neutral darkening for white text, without an added blue tint. The collapsed shelf keeps the same blur with lighter shading, darkening continuously as it opens. Whole label and upward player swipes open it; body/handle/list pulls settle by release velocity and distance. Once a gesture starts opening Queue, reversing only closes Queue and cannot dismiss the player. A downward pull from its top-scrolled list closes it; scrolling/flings/reordering retain ownership. |
| Header | Boxed numbered selector + delete X; play/pause and sort left, occurrence index/count above total duration center, playlist-save/More right. Save to new/existing playlist; Play resumes the browsed queue's cursor. |
| Selector | Rounded popup: numbered queues, highlighted browsing selection, separate playback marker, rename pencil, delete X, reorder and Remove all other queues. Selected long title marquees; other rows, closed selector and search prompts use single-line ellipsis. |
| Songs/actions | A subtly denser, textured frosted slab marks only the current queue occurrence, with a faint material edge; other browsing lists have no playback highlight. Occurrence menus and drag ordering remain available; information links close player/queue overlays and open the collection without changing playback. Queue More omits List view options; global Repeat/Shuffle stay in the player. Browsing/deletion behavior: [Product](product.md#queues-and-playback). |
| Return control | When the current occurrence is offscreen or covered by floating controls, fade in a Liquid Glass bubble pointing toward it. Ease it below the search pill or to the closest possible position. Keep its last direction throughout exit. During selection/drag, slide/fade this down so selection actions occupy the bottom. |

Retain the current complete visual while the next artwork/palette loads; prepare the actual playback neighbors without blocking audio. Horizontal cover swipes follow the finger, feathering adjacent covers through their blended color continuation. A short peek returns; sufficient travel or a directional flick commits the previewed visit once. Right swipes choose the actual previous visit independently of the Previous restart preference. Hold the prepared destination until playback/artwork catches up, then hand it over without a second wash; interrupted touches and invalidated previews return safely.

Buttons and autoplay use the same visible perspective carousel as artwork swipes; edge softness follows travel continuously in both directions. Identical decoded covers stay still. Full-background covers feather into their blended color continuation; floating covers keep separate edges while their backdrops crossfade. Reduced motion changes artwork without travel. Lyrics remain open across songs and crossfade when the next document is ready. Loading never exposes a white backing surface, blanks the player or replaces artwork prematurely. No routine loading/connection status text. Genuine playback failures use a rounded dismissible dialog with plain-language guidance and relevant Retry/Skip actions.

## Player actions

Hold an **empty slot** or use Settings to customize; occupied buttons never open the editor. With all slots filled, use Settings. Provide a slot preview, scrollable icon palette, smooth direct drag/drop with haptics, and tap alternatives. Centered layout uses balanced columns; non-centered uses a horizontal slot carousel. Two rows/Small change spaces; retain intentional gaps and hidden assignments when changing layout.

Offer functional information, editing, art, lyrics, playlist, pin, collection links, shuffle/repeat, count, ten-second seek and More actions. Default: Info/Add to playlist left, Repeat/Shuffle right. Info uses circled i; playlist uses list-plus. Information uses a frosted sheet with art, file/location, known technical details, and an Explore group of icon-bearing glass collection links. The compact frosted tag editor centers artwork above its checked metadata fields, with change/remove controls on the preview. Checked blank fields still remove tags. Image selection offers rounded recent previews when photo access is granted, or a short explanation and glass permission button otherwise. Gallery/files are icon-bearing glass actions separated from recents by an evenly spaced divider. A square crop with pan, pinch, zoom and reset remains required before review. Bulk writes offer Continue in background and Stop after current file; Settings → Library and tags → Tag jobs and recovery → Tag editing jobs shows progress and recent per-file outcomes.

Mode icons visibly reflect state without filled hitboxes or ripples: dim when off, full strength when active, visible 1 for Repeat one, distinct Stop/Next paused/Next queue indicators and accessible announcements. Hold Repeat for Simple/Advanced options; tapping returns to the ordinary cycle. Playback semantics live in [Product](product.md#queues-and-playback); keep the destination shelf extensible.

## Artwork and lyrics

- Default full-background cover: scale uniformly to screen width without side cropping, retain its vertical center, keep roughly the upper two thirds clear. Feather the lower third into broad curved color regions preserving left/right cover colors, extending above/below with gentle control shading. Avoid a straight fade to one average color. Alternative: floating cover over an artwork-colored matte/acrylic gradient with subtle non-tiled texture.
- Both layouts animate into one integrated lyrics state: no boundary or Lyrics heading; blend lyrics into the upper screen, lower metadata/transport, fade/slide extra actions away. Keep metadata above seek and transport accessible. Fade the artwork and its feathered edges uniformly. Artwork must reappear during held collapse, before release. Only lyrics mode shows the fading top-right lyrics button, which opens source selection.
- Default artwork tap toggles lyrics; hold opens View artwork / Change artwork / Configure tap action. Independently map tap/hold to lyrics, menu, viewer, change or nothing in Settings. Keep accessible alternatives. Viewer supports pinch/pan/reset and returns to the same player state; changes use the [shared single-song editor](product.md#metadata-and-artwork-editing).
- Follow real playback/seeks/queue changes with active-line emphasis, fluid movement and fading viewport edges, inspired by [Better Lyrics](https://github.com/better-lyrics/better-lyrics). Manual scrolling suspends following; a Liquid Glass directional bubble returns to the current line. Touch exploration starts with following off. Plain lyrics scroll normally. Keep source selection and honest empty/error states, without loading text/progress or tap ripples. [Source policy](product.md#lyrics-sources).

## Scanner and folders

More → Scan music opens the shared rounded dialog: Scan now, live progress/song count, Folders to scan and Metadata read failures. Its gear opens scanner configuration under Library and tags; Settings contains configuration, not manual scanning. Both routes share the folders page with names/paths, removal and Add folder. Metadata failures appear here, never as a persistent queue warning.

After starting, offer Continue in background and Cancel with completion/failure state. Explain actual permission/background restrictions and link directly to Localica's relevant Android settings page; do not promise to scroll another app's settings. Configuration: subfolders, minimum duration, per-folder relative exclusions and opt-in launch scanning. Adding a folder only grants access. Safety/lifecycle guarantees: [Structure](structure.md#file-operations).
