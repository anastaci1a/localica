# Interface contract

Use the supplied `localica-reference` screenshots for structure: Apple Music navigation/player, Musicolet lists/queues/scanner, and Poweramp action customization. This document owns visible behavior and UI defaults; [Product](product.md) owns media/playback semantics and [Structure](structure.md) owns implementation.

## Shared controls and motion

### Materials and controls

- Floating pills, mini-player, filter/search bars and circular controls share Liquid Glass: curved refraction, illuminated edges and crisp foregrounds. The Queue uses denser textured neutral frost; popups use the same material with light/dark tints. Related controls morph into their panels and return as one visible surface: creation, More, sorting, search and queue/lyric-source selectors.
- Selected media have a clear, untinted rounded lens, appearing/disappearing immediately until the next design review. Side-adjacent selections merge; diagonal gallery neighbors and unselected holes stay separate. Partial groups retain accessible state without a pane/checkmark. Inset album choices and preserve readable baselines.
- Sort/settings choices share a moving clear lens with tap/drag control; settings sliders and switches follow the [shared control specification](liquid-glass/specification.md#settings-sliders-and-switches). Use deeper glass for controls that morph into menus, carrying the same depth into their flyouts. The tab bar stays flatter because it retains its shape; Search/More follows the deeper profile because its surface opens a menu. Separate the actions in every two-button glass pill with a subtle vertical divider. Use rounded Apple Music-style icons, one shared original record glyph for albums, and attributed open-source transport artwork; omit unavailable actions. Action-menu width fits measured labels, icons and padding within the screen; returning flyouts retain the trigger’s ink/tint. Keep system pickers/permissions native and artwork/timed-lyric taps ripple-free.
- Corner tokens: small art 8 dp, cards 16, inputs/menus 20, large art 24, panels 28. Compact shelves/chips use pills; transport uses circles. Icons start at 24 dp with a unified icon/label/surface target of at least 48 dp; clip feedback to that shape.

### Motion and access

- Gestures track slow drags, reversals and interruptions, settling by distance/velocity. Keep the original owner until release: sliders, lyrics, reordering, carousels and overflow bars must not swipe the parent, even at an edge. Short-screen scrolling takes precedence; one gesture changes one surface level.
- Presses, morphs and selectors share interruptible deformation driven by acceleration and held pressure. Position stops at the destination; width/height/depth convey impact at approximately constant volume. Edge compression pins the contacting edge, saturates smoothly and relaxes on release. Contact flattens the lens; release rebounds with the same damping. Reduced motion removes deformation.
- Glass shape morphs remain visible for roughly 500 ms at normal speed and 350 ms with Faster animations; Reduce motion removes the morph. Other surfaces use about 300 ms and lyrics 260 ms. Retain exit drawing until handoff, but dismissed menus immediately release input. Full settings/editor/artwork pages slide/fade. Programmatic scrolling stops without overshoot; long journeys virtualize each frame’s crossed distance along one continuous ease and finish within about 500 ms. Touch cancels scrolling.
- Keep focus stable, accessible/tap alternatives available, and layouts usable in landscape, large fonts and short screens. Long titles pause, scroll and fade at their edges; reduced motion uses ellipsis. Shared glass text fields wrap labels and retain native editing semantics. A plain trailing X fades in only for nonempty text; clearing keeps or restores focus and the keyboard.

### Appearance and settings

Browsing/settings stay light, with a pink selected-tab accent, shared neutral gray for secondary text/inactive icons, and red for errors/destructive actions. Use Inter with compact tracking and bold mini-player titles; do not bundle Apple's system font. **Interface → Appearance** persistently adjusts gray, with reset to the darker default. Broader themes remain [planned](roadmap.md).

Settings categories are Interface, Playback and queues, Library and tags, About/licenses, with icons, summaries and chevrons; search appears only at the root. Both Back controls reverse the page entrance direction.

**Interface → Glass** provides live persistent controls and reset, with a draggable preview that compresses at its edges and morphs between a pill and dialog:

| Control | Range / treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Blur / white tint | Clear through frosted; defaults use light scattering/tint. |
| Shape damping / resting depth | Damping 2–40 (default 8); depth 0.6–2 (default 1.35). Footprint and lensing share the same response. |
| Shadow strength / spread | Off through visible; tight through broad; default very subtle and broad. |
| Adaptive contrast | Stable ink polarity and soft glyph-local white/black support preserve foreground readability; media selections remain untinted. |
| Navigation selector | Adjustable glass has independent blur/tint; Clear overlay refracts the finished bar/labels and disables but retains those values; Classic gray pill has no glass deformation. |

These controls affect floating glass. Queue frost remains independent.

## Navigation and Back

- **Destinations:** default Home + Library; keep at least one unique ordered tab. Available: Home, Library, Songs, Albums, Artists, Album Artists, Folders, Playlists, Queues, Most played. Four fit the normal phone shelf; wider sets or large fonts overflow without shrinking touch targets. Fitted cells accommodate the widest measured label with capsule padding.
- **Selector:** one lens responds immediately to taps and magnetically follows manual page swipes. Large icons/readable labels share an accent mask under the lens. Each newly tapped/dragged focus produces one system-respecting haptic. A different tab preserves its route/position; reselecting the settled current tab returns to its root and eases to the top, without history. Library reselection also clears its filter.
- **Dragging:** when tabs fit, dragging anywhere in the pill moves the magnetic selector; crossing into another destination changes pages without scrubbing the pager. Overflowing bars instead scroll on every drag and disable selector dragging. Clip scrolling contents to the capsule. Overdrag compresses the lens with diminishing resistance and stretches the outer pill's far edge, fixing the opposite edge and reducing height/depth; release restores it.
- **Page swipes:** require more horizontal intent than vertical lists, without changing carousel recognition/ownership. Follow tab order without wrapping. Preserve each tab's route, scroll, completed content and artwork. Headings travel with their pages, aligned to search/filter insets; detail headings crossfade. Commit visit history after settling.
- **Back:** first end focused text editing and hide keyboard/cursor while preserving the draft. Then dismiss topmost popup/dialog → visible queue/playlist selection → queue overlay, artwork viewer or lyrics → player → detail/search history → tab visits → exit. Detail/search return restores the parent query, first item and pixel offset after matching results load.
- **Lifecycle:** tab history defaults on; exit confirmation off. Disabling tab history preserves detail/search Back. Recreation restores state; fresh launch prefers Home, otherwise the first tab. Activity closure leaves playback running.

Tabs are 64 dp high; the mini-player and standalone circular controls are four fifths of that height (51.2 dp), excluding system insets. Mini-player artwork is 32 dp, vertically centered with a 16 dp leading inset. Its lens and menu-opening controls are deeper than the tab shelf/selector (1.3× versus 0.72× the configured resting depth); its compact title/artist use 13/11 sp with a 17 dp separation between line origins. Bottom floating surfaces share one shape-aware shadow without stacked overlap. Three aligned floating pills follow the Apple Music reference: full-width mini-player above flexible tabs at left and a divided Search/More pill with 28 dp icons at right. Search is immediately left of More; More offers Scan music and Settings; About/licenses lives only in Settings. Lists draw behind these controls, with enough content padding to reveal final items. Inside album, artist, Album Artist, playlist and folder details, tabs and utility controls leave; the mini-player moves down and remains available. Library/Playlists have an aligned circular + matching the mini-player's height; it morphs into its frosted creation menu and returns on dismissal, offering Create playlist and Import playlist from file ([import rules](product.md#library-and-playlists)).

## Browsing and search

- Use shared catalog/content/search templates. List/gallery settings inherit by list type with optional collection overrides and reset. Album catalogs and album contents are separate types. Gallery **Items per row** snaps to 1–5; preserve legacy presets until changed. Use smaller text/tighter padding at three or more columns; retain full-width galleries with an overlaid scrollbar.
- Outside queues and album details, pinch outward through List → five columns → four → three → two → one, and inward to reverse. Change one adjacent view per gesture, requiring all fingers to lift before another step. Pinch distance linearly targets item geometry, with a brief interruptible response filter to avoid recognition/fast-input jumps; reversing restores the source, including items virtualized out by the destination. Release settles to the source or destination and only then saves the view. Keep the nearest item at its vertical position and preserve selection. View controls remain the accessible alternative; reduced motion changes layout immediately.
- With no filter selected, Library combines collections: default Albums, Album Artists, Playlists; optionally Artists/Folders. Settings → Library and tags → Library filters owns visible filters, resting order and **Unfiltered Library collections**. Holding a filter opens a shortcut there. Empty filter/collection sets are valid; hiding the active filter clears selection. Global search retains all types. Labels use **Album Artist / Album Artists**.
- Covers: circles for Artist/Album Artist, rounded squares for albums/playlists; representative member artwork for collections. Reuse a tapped cover immediately in its detail header and crossfade its higher-resolution replacement. Keep the visible artwork of each tab and the previous two Back destinations. Show memory-cached covers immediately; newly decoded covers use a subtle loading sweep and 300 ms reveal, with static missing-cover placeholders and reduced-motion support. Gallery actions are on long-press, without overlaid menu buttons. Collection/nonmutable-song holds and plain row ellipses open the same anchored actions; mutable-song holds select, then moving the same finger starts reordering. Actions include: select songs, queue/playlist destinations and pinning; playlists additionally offer image, rename and delete. Playlist detail repeats these actions in its floating overflow control.
- Library root has no top search box: bottom Search opens a global-search page. Selecting a glass filter bubble moves it to the far left; the previous selection returns to its configured position, with neighbors yielding progressively and scrolling following the selected bubble. Tapping the selected filter clears it and restores its position; there is no All button. Keep configured order separate from this temporary arrangement. Start movement on tap. Immediately fade away the previous results; reveal the new results only with their matching saved sort/layout, independently of bubble movement.
- Album contents omit scoped search and its setting. Other lists can enable scoped quick search in the **View** tab of browse options. Search pills and filter bubbles share their height and vertical placement (40 dp visible inside a 48 dp target, 4 dp outer vertical inset; grow together for large text). Both float above a nonlinear list-edge fade with initial content below them. Search morphs the pill into one frosted foreground panel and reverses on close. Collapse only visible vacated header space, restoring its original scroll offset; already-scrolled content stays put. Global/local search are not main tabs.
- Search focuses input and raises the keyboard when its field is ready; a morph defers focus until its final approach. Typing edits one draft; IME submission or opening a result commits it. Each completed search retains query, scope, filters, sort, results position and identity. After editing ends, Back retraces searches without reopening the keyboard. Opening results/player preserves the source and popup positions. Mixed-result queues include only displayed song occurrences, in order.
- The entire divided Sort/View pill expands into one frosted panel, initially showing the tapped tab. A fixed icon-and-label glass tab header separates navigation from settings below. Keep the panel size constant between tabs; scroll-edge fades appear only where content is hidden. Both tabs share one draft: switching preserves edits; Save applies both and Cancel discards them. Choice controls follow the [shared staged/inline rules](liquid-glass/controls.md). Defaults: catalogs/search title ascending; album songs disc/track; playlists/queues saved order; recent newest; frequent most played; pins manual. Offer applicable title/count/duration/year/date/artist/Album Artist/composer/genre fields. Date added/modified start newest-first; Most played highest-first, all reversible. Rules for dates/counts: [Product](product.md#dates-and-listening-statistics).
- The **View** tab offers: presentation, Items per row, extra information, applicable grouping and scoped search, retaining inheritance/overrides. Overflowing lists of at least 40 items get a narrow edge scrollbar and accessible scroll action; show letter/year previews only during dragging under title/year sort.

## Collection details

- Album and artist pages use a full-width artwork header feathered into a color continuation behind the songs. Artwork travels at 65% of content speed with a continuous offset after the lazy header leaves view; reduced motion disables parallax. Choose black or white foregrounds from the artwork brightness. Sort and visibility share a glass pill where both are present; the whole pill opens the shared tabbed panel. Collection overflow opens the requested tab in one window and returns to the persistent three-dot anchor; empty lists show a quiet centered inset-style icon. Back and collection overflow are glass controls; the top color fade depends on scrolling past the artwork and is absent at the top.
- Detail entry immediately uses the cached cover and its colors, title and artist; crossfade the higher-resolution artwork/color continuation together. Newly loaded tracks reveal in a bounded sequential fade sweep, disabled by reduced motion.
- Album details stay in normal list presentation and ascending disc/track order, without Sort/View or pinch changes. Their bottom-right overflow shares playlist collection actions, excluding playlist-only edits. Album headers show the album/artist and a large Play control using the configured song-tap behavior. List rows use muted track numbers, regular song titles and quiet horizontal ellipses. Song-row overflow remains plain until its menu opens; top-level utility menus retain vertical dots.
- Artist and Album Artist pages use the newest album by tagged year, then library-added date, with stable identity as a tie-breaker; no artwork scraping. Below the header, a horizontal album multiselect filters the sorted song list. No explicit selection means All albums; selecting an album replaces All, and deselecting the last restores All. A combined glass Play/Shuffle pill acts on that displayed list. Shuffle randomizes the new queue’s saved order without enabling global traversal shuffle.

The Add to playlists panel combines covered existing-playlist rows with an inline new-name field; Add or keyboard submission applies all chosen destinations. Duplicate names disable Add, using the [playlist rules](product.md#library-and-playlists).

## Selection and reordering

- Holding a mutable playlist/queue song selects immediately; releasing keeps multiselect, and continued movement can start dragging without lifting. Other items use Select song / Select all songs in their shared menu. The bulk action panel slides/fades above the mini-player over 220 ms, independently of immediate item selection, retaining tabs. Normal collection taps navigate; song taps toggle membership while selection is active. Use the [shared selection treatment](#materials-and-controls).
- Retain source occurrence identities and selection order across routes, tabs, queries, sorting, rotation and backgrounding. Select-all appends in collection order; reselect appends last. Preserve distinct occurrences for playlists/queues and deduplicate files for editing. Fresh sessions start empty.
- Panel: All, Range, count, actions and a divided collapse/close glass pill; wrap/scroll within a bounded height. Close, mutable-list Back or successful actions exit. Cancel/failure/empty selection retains the mode; collapsing the panel retains selection. Remove-from-source requires one editable source. No separate selection-basket screen. Playlist chooser marks existing membership accessibly while allowing duplicates.
- In a complete, manual, unfiltered queue/playlist, hold-drag moves the held occurrence or selected occurrences from that source, preserving their source order even when nonconsecutive. Cross-source selections and sorted/filtered views cannot reorder.
- Lift visible selected rows into a shadowed Liquid Glass stack above selection controls/list clipping, sharing its material with Player actions dragging. The first source-order song is on top; at most two empty glass layers trail it, with a count badge for multiple items. Lifted content retains its actual list/gallery layout and lands on the rendered item bounds; material and trailing actions crossfade without shrinking the content or jumping at handoff. Track the finger directly; keep a one-row insertion gap, ease surrounding rows, edge-scroll and smoothly unpack on release.
- Hide the action panel throughout dragging/landing. A Liquid Glass foreground X circle remains above the stack. Hover tints it red with one firm haptic; leaving gives a lighter tick, honoring system settings. Release there cancels and restores the panel. Other drops, including an unchanged insertion position, finish selection after successful landing. System interruption or failed persistence restores order, selection and the panel; all landings crossfade into normal rows and preserve playback cursors.

## Player and Queue drawer

The mini-player appears whenever an active queue has a current song, including paused playback. Inset its artwork/title together inside the leading curve; use black filled Previous/Play/Pause/Next icons. The settled Play triangle uses a single filled contour; it morphs into Pause; skips use two rounded triangles without an end bar, with a staggered directional nudge. Secondary metadata defaults to Album Artist with Artist fallback; Artist-first is configurable. The body is one rounded tap target; transport remains independent.

Tap/swipe up expands one shared outline containing glass, artwork, text and transport. Glass grows blurrier through expansion; the final artwork color continuation blends in later and becomes opaque near full size, without passing through a darker average-color backing. Full-background covers soften at their perimeter while keeping the center sharp, then merge into the continuation; floating covers keep defined edges. The growing cover is weighted slightly later to clear the title; its feather never drops away at the final handoff. All blends reverse with the gesture.

Metadata/seek/actions/lyrics have 32 dp side insets. Order: artwork → metadata → seek/times → customizable actions → transport → Queue drawer; no volume controls. Album metadata fades in and seek/actions appear late, while transport persists throughout. Normal metadata and controls sit below the cover with balanced space above Queue; lyrics mode lowers them further and centers transport between the seek track itself and the collapsed Queue shelf’s top.

While scrubbing, show the same foreground X target as mutable-list dragging, above the Queue shelf. Releasing on X discards the preview and restores the live playback position without a seek or pause; other releases commit the preview.

A 72 dp translucent handle appears late in expansion below the safe top inset. During collapse it keeps the same inset from the moving top edge and fades early, avoiding control overlap; its accessible tap target and the top-left glass down arrow collapse the player. Main tabs leave downward. Playback-starting song taps expand by default (configurable); add-next/append do not. Swipe down/Back restores underlying browse state. Opening commits at 240 dp/s or about 22 dp of travel; player and Queue closing share 300 dp/s or 72 dp, with progress deciding otherwise. Both remain interruptible during settling; player collapse slows into its resting position. During collapse, artwork and its color continuation resolve together before detaching; no stretched backing cover or dark browse flash.

Retain the current complete visual while the next artwork/palette loads; prepare the actual playback neighbors without blocking audio. Album-art swiping can be disabled independently in Settings → Interface → Player (enabled by default). Enabled horizontal cover swipes follow the finger, feathering adjacent covers through their blended color continuation. A short peek returns; sufficient travel or a directional flick commits the previewed visit once on release. Re-grabbing a settling carousel preserves its displayed position; neither settling nor artwork loading disables horizontal ownership. Right swipes choose the actual previous visit independently of the Previous restart preference. Hold the prepared destination until playback/artwork catches up, then hand it over without a second wash; interrupted touches and invalidated previews return safely.

Buttons and autoplay default to a symmetric blur-through: soften the outgoing cover, blend near peak blur, then resolve the incoming cover. Settings offers the perspective carousel as an alternative; its edge softness follows travel continuously in both directions. Identical decoded covers stay still. Full-background covers feather into their blended color continuation; floating covers keep separate edges while their backdrops crossfade. Reduced motion uses a short fade without blur or travel. Lyrics remain open between songs with local sources and crossfade their documents; a completed lookup with no sources closes lyrics. Loading never exposes a white backing surface, blanks the player or replaces artwork prematurely. No routine loading/connection status text. Genuine playback failures use a rounded dismissible dialog with plain-language guidance and relevant Retry/Skip actions.

## Player actions

Hold an **empty slot** or use Settings to customize; occupied buttons never open the editor. With all slots filled, use Settings. Provide a slot preview, scrollable icon palette, smooth direct drag/drop with haptics, and tap alternatives. Centered layout uses balanced columns; non-centered uses a horizontal slot carousel. Two rows/Small change spaces; retain intentional gaps and hidden assignments when changing layout.

Offer functional information, editing, art, lyrics, playlist, pin, collection links, shuffle/repeat, count, ten-second seek and More actions. Default: Info/Add to playlist left, Repeat/Shuffle right. Info uses circled i; playlist uses list-plus.

- Information: frosted sheet with tappable artwork opening the viewer, file/location, known technical details and an Explore group of icon-bearing glass collection links. Popup controls and secondary text share the material’s light/dark palette.
- Tag editor: compact frosted panel, centered artwork above checked fields, change/remove controls on the preview. Follow [editing and crop rules](product.md#metadata-and-artwork-editing); the cropper offers pan, pinch, zoom and reset.
- Image picker: rounded recent previews when access is granted; otherwise a short explanation and glass permission button. An evenly spaced divider separates these from icon-bearing glass Gallery/Files actions.
- Bulk jobs: Continue in background and Stop after current file; **Settings → Library and tags → Tag jobs and recovery → Tag editing jobs** shows progress/recent per-file outcomes.

Mode icons visibly reflect state without filled hitboxes or ripples: dim when off, full strength when active, visible 1 for Repeat one, distinct Stop/Next paused/Next queue indicators and accessible announcements. Hold Repeat for Simple/Advanced options; tapping returns to the ordinary cycle. Playback semantics live in [Product](product.md#queues-and-playback); keep the destination shelf extensible.

## Artwork and lyrics

- Default full-background cover: scale uniformly to screen width without side cropping, retain its vertical center, keep roughly the upper two thirds clear. Feather the lower third into broad curved color regions preserving left/right cover colors, extending above/below with gentle control shading. Avoid a straight fade to one average color. Alternative: floating cover over an artwork-colored matte/acrylic gradient with subtle non-tiled texture.
- Both layouts animate into one integrated lyrics state: no boundary or Lyrics heading; blend lyrics into the upper screen, lower metadata/transport, fade/slide extra actions away. Keep metadata above seek and transport accessible. Fade the artwork and its feathered edges uniformly. Artwork must reappear during held collapse, before release. Gate every lyrics entry point, including mapped artwork actions, on a discovered embedded or matching-file source; preserve assigned action slots when unavailable. An open page may wait for the next song’s lookup, but closes if no source is found. When lyrics exist, a top-right glass button opens them. As they appear, that same bubble widens into a divided pill: the persistent lyrics icon moves left and becomes source selection; a crossed-out eye on the right hides lyrics.
- Default artwork tap toggles lyrics; hold opens View artwork / Change artwork / Configure tap action. Independently map tap/hold to lyrics, menu, viewer, change or nothing in Settings. Keep accessible alternatives. Viewer supports pinch/pan/reset and returns to the same player state; Change artwork opens image selection directly, then the shared artwork-only review/write flow for the current song in the [shared single-song editor](product.md#metadata-and-artwork-editing).
- Follow real playback/seeks/queue changes with active-line emphasis, fluid movement and fading viewport edges, inspired by [Better Lyrics](https://github.com/better-lyrics/better-lyrics). Manual scrolling suspends following; a Liquid Glass directional bubble returns to the current line. Touch exploration starts with following off. Plain lyrics scroll normally. Keep source selection without loading text/progress or tap ripples; unavailable lyrics do not open an empty panel. [Source policy](product.md#lyrics-sources).

## Scanner and folders

More → Scan music opens the shared rounded dialog: Scan now, live progress/song count, Folders to scan and Metadata read failures. Its gear opens scanner configuration under Library and tags; Settings contains configuration, not manual scanning. Both routes share the folders page with names/paths, removal and Add folder. Metadata failures appear here, never as a persistent queue warning.

After starting, offer Continue in background and Cancel with completion/failure state. Explain actual permission/background restrictions and link directly to Localica's relevant Android settings page; do not promise to scroll another app's settings. Configuration: subfolders, minimum duration, per-folder relative exclusions and opt-in launch scanning. Adding a folder only grants access. Safety/lifecycle guarantees: [Structure](structure.md#file-operations).
