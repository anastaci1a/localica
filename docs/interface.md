# Interface contract

Use the supplied `localica-reference` screenshots for structure: Apple Music navigation/player, Musicolet lists/queues/scanner, and Poweramp action customization. This document owns visible behavior and UI defaults; [Product](product.md) owns media/playback semantics and [Structure](structure.md) owns implementation.

## Shared controls and motion

### Materials and controls

- Floating pills, mini-player, filter/search bars and circular controls share Liquid Glass: curved refraction, illuminated edges and crisp foregrounds. The Queue uses denser textured neutral frost; popups use the same material and inherited adaptive contrast. Related controls morph into their panels and return as one visible surface: creation, More, sorting, search and queue/lyric-source selectors.
- Selected media have a clear, untinted rounded lens, appearing/disappearing immediately until the next design review. Side-adjacent selections merge; diagonal gallery neighbors and unselected holes stay separate. Partial groups retain accessible state without a pane/checkmark. Inset album choices and preserve readable baselines.
- Sort/settings choices share a moving clear lens with tap/drag control; settings sliders and switches follow the [shared control specification](liquid-glass/specification.md#settings-sliders-and-switches). Use deeper glass for controls that morph into menus, carrying the same depth into their flyouts. The tab bar stays flatter because it retains its shape; Search/More follows the deeper profile because its surface opens a menu. Separate the actions in every two-button glass pill with a subtle vertical divider. Use rounded Apple Music-style icons, one shared original record glyph for albums, and attributed open-source transport artwork; omit unavailable actions. Action-menu width fits measured labels, icons and padding within the screen; returning flyouts gradually restore the shared trigger foreground with its original ink/tint. Keep system pickers/permissions native and artwork/timed-lyric taps ripple-free.
- Corner tokens: small art 8 dp, cards 16, inputs/menus 20, large art 24, panels 28. Compact shelves/chips use pills; transport uses circles. Icons start at 24 dp with a unified icon/label/surface target of at least 48 dp; clip feedback to that shape.
- Scrollable popup bodies fade content only at edges hiding more content, fading the affordance away as each boundary is reached. Bodies that fit have no edge fade. Keep the shell and fixed title/footer outside the body mask; oversized chrome may scroll when necessary to keep actions reachable.

### Motion and access

- Gestures track slow drags, reversals and interruptions, settling by distance/velocity. Controls show contact immediately but defer movement ownership: fast drags along an available parent scroll axis scroll without activating/changing the control; slow deliberate adjustment or a held drag retains control ownership. Perpendicular scrolling remains available. Once resolved, retain the owner through reversal and edges; cancellation never commits. Opening a modal cancels background contacts and flings, rolls back uncommitted pinch/reorder previews, and discards their later releases; dismissal permits fresh gestures, never resumes old fingers. Only the top modal accepts interaction, including nested or separately declared dialogs. Shared action buttons, transport/player actions and filter bubbles give exactly one weak system-respecting pulse per successful activation, including accessible activation; disabled actions, cancelled presses and programmatic state changes stay silent. Multi-select media and artist album choices retain scrolling. Lyrics, reordering, carousels and overflow bars must not swipe the parent once acquired; one gesture changes one surface level.
- Horizontal/vertical choices and fitting navigation preview the lens at any touched option without committing or pulsing. A deliberate hold or owned axial drag continues from that contact. Selectors, settings sliders and switch thumbs give weak owned engagement, one weak tick per distinct discrete step/Boolean transition, and a strong genuine release; cancellation has no release pulse. Ordinary tap, keyboard and accessible activation give one weak pulse at the input owner, including the full-width switch row. Continuous sliders have no step ticks; pending contact, parent scrolling and external/animated state changes stay silent. Parent scrolling cancels preview/activation and retains ownership through direction changes. Initial cross-axis travel alone must not disable later axial manipulation when no competing scroll wins.
- Presses, morphs and selectors share interruptible deformation driven by acceleration and held pressure. Position stops at the destination; width/height/depth convey impact at approximately constant volume. Navigation and horizontal/vertical choice tracks share overdrag geometry: the track stretches along travel within a shared 4 dp limit independent of length, its contents deform and realign proportionally, and the selector’s gap to the moving wall progressively closes. A held selector retains its resting cross-axis extent; touching an already moving selector eases from the displayed contour. Contact corners stay circular and concentric with the enclosing track; the free end absorbs compression. Contact flattens the lens. Release couples width and height in opposite phase with bounded physical excursions; the enclosing wall and lens share the configured timing and retain their visible contour on re-touch. One selector presentation owns the released geometry while optical depth continues independently. Either the app’s Reduced motion setting or Android’s disabled animator duration scale removes shared deformation, edge pressure and rebound. Changes apply while open; restoring Android animations resumes motion only when the app option is off.
- Glass shape morphs remain visible for roughly 500 ms at normal speed and 350 ms with Faster animations; Reduce motion removes the morph. Other surfaces use about 300 ms and lyrics 260 ms. Retain exit drawing until handoff, but dismissed menus immediately release input. Reopening a retained, prepared menu immediately reclaims its surface and shadow; newly mounted windows prepare before hiding the trigger. Full settings/editor/artwork pages slide/fade. Programmatic scrolling stops without overshoot; long journeys virtualize each frame’s crossed distance along one continuous ease and finish within about 500 ms. Touch cancels scrolling.
- Active/inactive and enabled/disabled control appearances activate over 180 ms and deactivate over 270 ms (120/180 ms with Faster animations), with no onset delay; icon badges crossfade in fixed bounds. State, accessibility and input availability change immediately, reversals continue from the visible appearance, and Reduce motion snaps to the result. Keep existing transport morphs and gesture-driven travel.
- Keep focus stable, accessible/tap alternatives available, and layouts usable in landscape, large fonts and short screens. Adjacent glass actions retain natural label widths and logical order; overflow offers explicit previous/next controls without changing button-owned drags or shrinking text. Long titles pause, scroll and fade at their edges; reduced motion uses ellipsis. Shared glass text fields wrap labels and retain native editing semantics. A plain trailing X fades in only for nonempty text; clearing keeps or restores focus and the keyboard.

### Appearance and settings

Browsing/settings stay light, with a pink selected-tab accent, shared neutral gray for secondary text/inactive icons, and red for errors/destructive actions. Use Inter with compact tracking and bold mini-player titles; do not bundle Apple's system font. **Interface → Appearance** persistently adjusts gray, with reset to the darker default. Broader themes remain [planned](roadmap.md).

Settings categories are Interface, Playback and queues, Library and tags, About/licenses, with icons, summaries and chevrons; search appears only at the root. Both Back controls reverse the page entrance direction.

**Interface → Glass** provides live persistent controls and reset, with a draggable preview that compresses at its edges and morphs between a pill and dialog:

| Control | Range / treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Blur / white tint | Clear through frosted; defaults use light scattering/tint. |
| Shape damping / resting depth | Damping 2–40 (default 8); depth 0.6–2 (default 1.35). Footprint and lensing share the same response. |
| Shadow strength / spread | Off through visible; tight through broad; default very subtle and broad. |
| Adaptive contrast | Page-wide ink polarity; background brightness trim 0–50% (default 25%) and weighting from even to highlight/shadow-focused. White icons darken highlights; dark icons lift shadows. Foreground and media selections remain unfiltered; dark artwork selects light ink, light artwork selects dark ink. The player’s blurred artwork extension uses the same trim/weight, without a separate bottom-darkening gradient. |
| Navigation selector | Adjustable glass has independent blur/tint; Clear overlay has 0–50% darkening (default off) and optional icon/text refraction (default off). Classic gray pill has adjustable opacity (default 10%) and no glass deformation. |
| Sliders and switches | White opaque resting thumbs morph into clear unblurred glass on contact; independent expansion 1–2× (default 1.75×). |

Adaptive contrast also applies to player color continuations, Queue frost and nested panels; all inherit the page’s artwork-selected ink polarity. Blur and texture retain their own material character.

## Navigation and Back

- **Destinations:** default Home + Library; keep at least one unique ordered tab. Available: Home, Library, Songs, Albums, Artists, Album Artists, Folders, Playlists and Queues; Most played remains a sort/statistic, not a tab. Four fit the normal phone shelf; wider sets or large fonts overflow without shrinking touch targets. Fitted cells accommodate the widest measured label with capsule padding.
- **Selector:** one lens responds immediately to taps and magnetically follows manual page swipes. Large icons/readable labels share an accent mask under the lens. Direct taps, including reselection, and owned drags use the shared selector haptics; cancellation is silent. Page swipes, programmatic/settling tracking and overflow scrolling produce no haptics. A different tab preserves its route/position; reselecting the settled current tab returns to its root and eases to the top, without history. Library reselection first scrolls to the top while retaining its filter; a separate tap while already at the top clears the filter.
- **Dragging:** fitting navigation uses the shared selector pickup above. Overflowing navigation permits lens dragging only from its visible moving lens; other origins scroll hidden tabs, while taps still select. Clip scrolling contents to the capsule. Shared overdrag fixes the opposite wall; overflow keeps its undeformed viewport. Contact, keyboard focus and settling raise the complete tab pill above neighboring utility controls without changing hit bounds.
- **Page swipes:** require at least twice as much horizontal as vertical intent, without changing carousel recognition/ownership. Follow tab order without wrapping. Preserve each tab's route, scroll, completed content and artwork. Headings travel with their pages, aligned to search/filter insets; detail headings crossfade. Commit visit history after settling.
- **Back:** first end focused text editing and hide keyboard/cursor while preserving the draft. Then dismiss topmost popup/dialog → visible queue/playlist selection → queue overlay, artwork viewer or lyrics → player → current main-tab list to the top → applicable list filter reset → detail/search history → tab visits → exit. Each press takes one applicable step; list scrolling/filter reset also applies within details, without returning to the tab root. Detail/search return restores the parent query, first item and pixel offset after matching results load.
- **Lifecycle:** tab history defaults on; exit confirmation off. Disabling tab history preserves detail/search Back. Recreation restores state; fresh launch prefers Home, otherwise the first tab. Activity closure leaves playback running.

Tabs are 64 dp high; the mini-player and standalone circular controls are four fifths of that height (51.2 dp), excluding system insets. Mini-player artwork is 32 dp, vertically centered with a 16 dp leading inset. Its lens and menu-opening controls are deeper than the tab shelf/selector (1.3× versus 0.72× the configured resting depth); its compact title/artist use 13/11 sp with a 17 dp separation between line origins. Bottom floating surfaces share one shape-aware shadow without stacked overlap. Three aligned floating pills follow the Apple Music reference: full-width mini-player above flexible tabs at left and a divided Search/More pill with 28 dp icons at right. Search is immediately left of More; More offers Scan music and Settings; About/licenses lives only in Settings. Lists draw behind these controls, with enough content padding to reveal final items. Inside album, artist, Album Artist, playlist and folder details, tabs and utility controls leave; the mini-player moves down and remains available. Library/Playlists have an aligned circular + matching the mini-player's height; it morphs into its frosted creation menu and returns on dismissal, offering Create playlist and Import playlist from file ([import rules](product.md#library-and-playlists)).

## Browsing and search

- Use shared catalog/content/search templates. Dedicated catalog tabs and corresponding Library filters share sort/view preferences, without page overrides. Existing Library filter overrides take precedence during migration. Collections and search retain optional overrides and reset; search text does not create another override. Album catalogs and contents are separate types. List **Size** offers Smallest, Small, Compact, Normal (default), and Large (18 sp titles); album, playlist and queue contents also offer Tracklist between Compact and Normal. Tracklist uses a track number (displayed position outside albums) beside the normal title/subtitle layout, without a cover. Normal/Large covers match Gallery 5/3 respectively. Compact keeps its fixed row pitch and text size, sizing leading artwork to Normal’s gap-to-art ratio up to the inner row height, with proportionate corner rounding; Small/Smallest omit artwork; Smallest uses 60% of the ordinary between-row padding while keeping its text, inner row height and horizontal spacing. These three dense sizes keep one-line extra information immediately before the overflow, with smaller single-line titles; metadata moves between trailing and subtitle positions through the shared layout animation. Long text ellipsizes, and larger system fonts grow rows. Normal/Large halve the inter-cover gap, retaining cover sizes and horizontal geometry. Gallery **Items per row** snaps to 1–5; preserve legacy presets until changed. Use smaller text/tighter padding at three or more columns, with an overlaid scrollbar.
- In all lists, including queues and album details, pinch outward through Smallest list → Small list → Compact list → Tracklist where offered → Normal list → Large list → Gallery 5 → 4 → 3 → 2 → 1, and inward to reverse. Change one adjacent view per gesture, requiring all fingers to lift before another step. Pinch distance linearly targets geometry with a brief interruptible response filter; reversing restores items virtualized out by the destination. Keep decoded artwork visible through source capture, easing and release; resolution upgrades blend without exposing the background. Release settles before saving. Anchor the scroll range at the top/bottom when already there; otherwise preserve the content under the pinch midpoint. Keep equal outer margins and selection across modes. View controls remain the accessible alternative; reduced motion changes layout immediately.
- With no filter selected, Library combines collections: default Albums, Album Artists, Playlists; optionally Artists/Folders. Settings → Library and tags → Library filters owns visible filters, resting order and **Unfiltered Library collections**. Holding a filter opens a shortcut there. Empty filter/collection sets are valid; hiding the active filter clears selection. Global search retains all types. Labels use **Album Artist / Album Artists**.
- Initial browsing waits for every catalog source used by the current view, never for artwork bytes. Fill the viewport with geometry-matched neutral artwork/title/information skeletons using one shimmer phase; omit unsupported artwork/information slots. Artist-only views start circular. Reveal ready rows at a fixed radial speed from the top-left, fading text/artwork over fading shapes; mixed-view artist placeholders and artwork share one eased square-to-circle contour. Reserve supported multiline height through the complete row reveal, then ease away only unused space. Cached returns do not replay loading; reduced motion removes shimmer/reveal/reflow motion.
- Covers: circles for Artist/Album Artist, rounded squares for albums/playlists; automatic collection covers use the first displayed member under that collection’s saved sort override or inherited contents sort, including album grouping. Playlist/queue covers follow their persisted occurrence order; explicit custom covers take precedence. A first member without artwork keeps the placeholder instead of borrowing a later member’s cover. Reuse a tapped cover immediately in its detail header and crossfade its higher-resolution replacement. Keep the visible artwork of each tab and the previous two Back destinations. Show memory-cached covers immediately; newly decoded covers use a subtle loading sweep and 300 ms reveal, with static missing-cover placeholders and reduced-motion support. Gallery actions are on long-press, without overlaid menu buttons. Collection/nonmutable-song holds and plain row ellipses open the same anchored actions; mutable-song holds select, then moving the same finger starts reordering. Actions include: select songs, queue/playlist destinations and pinning; playlists additionally offer image, rename and delete. The image action sets a custom cover when absent and removes it when present, restoring representative member artwork. Playlist detail repeats these actions in its floating overflow control.
- Scrolled song lists offer a bottom-center return-to-top control without clearing filters or changing the current list; Home returns its vertical list without resetting the pinned carousel. Active Queue uses its current-occurrence following control instead (below). Return and song-drag cancel controls share the collection utility row above bottom chrome/mini-player. Return and drag/seek-cancel controls fade in while rising, continuing through reversals; Faster animations shortens the entrance and Reduce motion snaps. Moving cancel hit regions follow the visible target, and hidden or replaced controls immediately relinquish input.
- Library root has no top search box: bottom Search opens a global-search page. Selecting a glass filter bubble moves it to the far left; the previous selection returns to its configured position, with neighbors yielding progressively and scrolling following the selected bubble. Tapping the selected filter clears it and restores its position; there is no All button. Keep configured order separate from this temporary arrangement. Start movement on tap. Immediately fade away the previous results; reveal the new results only with their matching saved sort/layout, independently of bubble movement.
- Album contents omit scoped search and its setting. Other lists can enable scoped quick search in the **View** tab of browse options. Search pills and filter bubbles share their height and vertical placement (40 dp visible inside a 48 dp target, 4 dp outer vertical inset; grow together for large text). Both float above a nonlinear list-edge fade with initial content below them. Search morphs the pill into one frosted foreground panel and reverses on close. Collapse only visible vacated header space, restoring its original scroll offset; already-scrolled content stays put. Global/local search are not main tabs.
- Scoped search focuses its field as soon as its layout is ready, resolves keyboard placement, then eases once directly above it; absent or floating keyboards cannot block opening. The panel grows upward with its field at the top, without scaling. Actual dismissal by empty Back or outside tap begins one direct return to the home pill while the keyboard hides independently. Nonempty Back first ends editing; the retained panel centers and adds 20% height within safe margins. Child menus freeze the parent’s position until return. Actual dismissal clears the scoped session; temporary covering/result navigation preserves it. Scoped results omit subtitles. Initial search results and filter changes reveal visible rows sequentially. Typing edits one draft; its editor session survives completed-query-to-draft transitions while result/history state remains route-owned. Back or actual dismissal retires the editor and its callbacks. IME submission or opening a result commits the draft. Each completed search retains query, scope, filters, sort, results position and identity. After editing ends, Back retraces searches without reopening the keyboard. Opening results/player preserves the source and popup positions. Mixed-result queues include only displayed song occurrences, in order.
- The entire divided Sort/View pill expands into one frosted panel, initially showing the tapped tab. A fixed icon-and-label glass tab header separates navigation from settings below. Keep the panel size constant across tabs and supported option states. Measure inert faces for the complete body, title and action-footer alternatives; wrap their maximum natural width within the shared window cap. Reserve title/footer space before bounding body height; do not use a separate screen-percentage cap. Both tabs share one draft: switching preserves edits; Save applies both and Cancel discards them. Choice controls follow the [shared staged/inline rules](liquid-glass/controls.md). Defaults: catalogs/search title ascending; album songs disc/track; playlists/queues saved order; recent newest; frequent most played; pins manual. Offer applicable title/count/duration/year/date/artist/Album Artist/composer/genre fields. Date added/modified start newest-first; Most played highest-first, all reversible. Rules for dates/counts: [Product](product.md#dates-and-listening-statistics).
- Extra information starts with None, then Follow sort order. Unfiltered Library and global search additionally offer Automatic between them and default to it when no choice is saved. Automatic inherits each row type’s catalog/filter extra information and, for Follow sort order, that type’s own sort. Changes to those settings refresh mixed rows; explicit saved choices retain their meaning.
- Collection/search overrides use **Save only for this list** above Cancel/Save; main catalog tabs have no override. In queues/playlists the switch belongs only to View. Revert uses shared glass emergence/disappearance in a reserved slot and disappears whenever the physical order matches the opening snapshot, including after two reversals. Manual restores the saved baseline. Sort by and Extra information use the same staged selector trigger and frosted dialog. View offers presentation, list Size or gallery Items per row, extra information and applicable scoped search. Overflowing lists of at least 40 items get a narrow edge scrollbar and accessible scroll action; show letter/year previews only during dragging under title/year sort.

- Album details default to Tracklist presentation and ascending disc/track order, with Sort/View and pinch alternatives. Their bottom-right overflow shares playlist collection actions, excluding playlist-only edits. Album headers show the album/artist and a large Play control using the configured song-tap behavior. Smallest/Small/Compact/Tracklist album rows use muted track numbers instead of covers; Normal/Large/Gallery show ordinary covers without track numbers. Existing album Normal preferences migrate once to Tracklist; an explicit new Normal choice is retained. Keep regular song titles and quiet horizontal ellipses. Song-row overflow remains plain until its menu opens; top-level utility menus retain vertical dots.

## Selection and reordering

- Holding a mutable playlist/queue song selects immediately; its new action panel waits for release, so continued movement into dragging never flashes it. Existing panels retain their state; accessibility selection without a pointer shows the panel immediately. Other items use Select song / Select all songs in their shared menu. The bulk action panel slides/fades above the mini-player over 220 ms, independently of immediate item selection, retaining tabs. Normal collection taps navigate; song taps toggle membership while selection is active. Use the [shared selection treatment](#materials-and-controls).
- Retain source occurrence identities and selection order across routes, tabs, queries, sorting, rotation and backgrounding. Select-all appends in collection order; reselect appends last. Preserve distinct occurrences for playlists/queues and deduplicate files for editing. Fresh sessions start empty.
- Panel: All, Range, count, actions and a divided collapse/close glass pill; wrap/scroll within a bounded height. Close, mutable-list Back or successful actions exit. Cancel/failure/empty selection retains the mode; collapsing the panel retains selection. Remove-from-source requires one editable source. No separate selection-basket screen. Playlist chooser marks existing membership accessibly while allowing duplicates.
- In a complete, manual, unfiltered queue/playlist, hold-drag moves the held occurrence or selected occurrences from that source, preserving their source order even when nonconsecutive. Cross-source selections and filtered views cannot reorder; applying a saved-order sort does not disable subsequent dragging.
- Lift visible selected rows into a shadowed Liquid Glass stack above selection controls/list clipping, sharing its material with Player actions dragging. The first source-order song is on top; at most two empty glass layers trail it, with a count badge for multiple items. Lifted content retains its actual list/gallery layout and lands on the rendered item bounds; material and trailing actions crossfade without shrinking the content or jumping at handoff. Track the finger directly; keep a one-row insertion gap, ease surrounding rows, edge-scroll and smoothly unpack on release. Playback checkpoints and unrelated refreshes retain an owned contact; route or source occurrence-order changes cancel it, and commits reject an intervening source-order change.
- Hide the action panel throughout dragging/landing. Floating controls share its animated visible occupancy, with an 8 dp gap above the panel, and return above remaining bottom chrome as it leaves. A Liquid Glass foreground X circle remains above the stack in the shared bottom control row. Hover tints it red with one firm haptic; leaving gives a lighter tick, honoring system settings. Release there cancels and restores the panel. Other drops, including an unchanged insertion position, finish selection after successful landing. System interruption or failed persistence restores order, selection and the panel; all landings crossfade into normal rows and preserve playback cursors.

## Player and Queue drawer

The mini-player appears whenever an active queue has a current song, including paused playback. Inset its artwork/title together inside the leading curve; use black filled Previous/Play/Pause/Next icons. The settled Play triangle uses a single filled contour; it morphs into Pause; skips use two rounded triangles without an end bar, with a staggered directional nudge. Secondary metadata defaults to Album Artist with Artist fallback; Artist-first is configurable. The body is one rounded tap target; transport remains independent.

Tap/swipe up expands one shared outline containing glass, artwork, text and transport. The expanded player follows device-reported corner radii; absent or unavailable physical geometry stays square. Queue keeps the shared rounded panel contour for its material, content clip and border throughout opening, closing and reversal, independently of physical window clipping. The entire Queue layer stays clipped inside the player’s moving rounded outline during expansion, collapse and interruption; its lower edge is revealed only as that outline reaches it. Glass grows blurrier through expansion; the final artwork color continuation blends in later and becomes opaque near full size, without passing through a darker average-color backing. Full-background covers soften at their perimeter while keeping the center sharp, then merge into the continuation; floating covers keep defined edges. The growing cover is weighted slightly later to clear the title; its feather never drops away at the final handoff. All blends reverse with the gesture.

Metadata/seek/actions/lyrics have 32 dp side insets. Order: artwork → metadata → seek/times → customizable actions → transport → Queue drawer; no volume controls. Album metadata fades in and seek/actions appear late, while transport persists throughout. During collapse, seek and the action row have distinct fly-down/fade curves between the unchanged title and transport motion; actions move slightly faster than seek. Normal metadata and controls sit below the cover with balanced space above Queue; lyrics mode lowers them further and centers transport between the seek track itself and the collapsed Queue shelf’s top.

While scrubbing, show the same foreground X target as mutable-list dragging, above the Queue shelf. Entering and leaving X ease symmetrically between live playback and the current finger position, continuing through reversals; Faster animations shortens the transition and Reduce motion snaps. An owned seek drag gives one weak engagement pulse and a strong genuine release outside X, with no continuous-position ticks. Entering X gives the shared firm pulse and leaving gives a weak tick; releasing there or cancelling discards the preview without a seek, pause or release pulse. Tap and accessible changes give one weak activation. Pending contact, parent scrolling, unrelated keys and live/programmatic playback tracking stay silent; feedback honors system settings. Other genuine releases commit the finger position and retain that visual target through asynchronous seek acknowledgement, without exposing a stale playback or contact-start frame; cancellation and obsolete media generations cannot commit it.

Title and artist default to content-based text direction, preserving explicit direction overrides, and use independently sized, longer soft edge fades throughout player expansion/collapse, never switching to ellipses mid-transition. Reduce motion retains the static ellipsis treatment. Pause ordinary marquee during movement; if offset, immediately ease to the beginning by the shortest path (including forward wrap). Keep the leading fade fully present until arrival, then ease it out. After the player settles and the normal delay passes, ease the leading fade in before restarting marquee. Preserve these phases independently for each overflowing line.

A 72 dp translucent handle appears late in expansion below the safe top inset, using the same ink polarity as text and icons. During collapse it keeps the same inset from the moving top edge and fades early, avoiding control overlap; its accessible tap target and the top-left glass down arrow collapse the player. Main tabs leave downward. Playback-starting song taps expand by default (configurable); add-next/append do not. Swipe down/Back restores underlying browse state. Opening commits at 240 dp/s or about 22 dp of travel; player and Queue closing share 300 dp/s or 72 dp, with progress deciding otherwise. Both remain interruptible during settling; player collapse slows into its resting position. During collapse, artwork and its color continuation resolve together before detaching; no stretched backing cover or dark browse flash.
