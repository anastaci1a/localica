# Interface contract

Build the lasting layout and interactions before applying liquid-glass materials. This is the agreed target; the roadmap identifies the next delivery checkpoint.

## Navigation and player

Default main tabs are Home and Library. Users can add, remove, and reorder Home, Library, Songs, Albums, Artists, Album Artists, Folders, Playlists, Queues, and Most-played Songs; retain at least one and disallow duplicates. Uniform icons are primary, with small labels and accessible touch targets. Fit tabs normally; scroll only on overflow. Whole-library Search stays immediately left of More at the right. More contains scan, settings, help/about, and close. Swipe configured tabs in order without wrapping; preserve child carousel and overflow-bar gesture ownership until release. Commit tab history only when settling. Library and Playlists have a floating + menu with Create playlist and Import playlist from file. Import local M3U/M3U8 and PLS through the system file picker, preserve order/duplicates, review unresolved entries, and never silently match ambiguous names or fetch remote URLs.

Swiping tabs retains per-tab routes and scroll positions. Every tab-button tap returns to that tab’s root and eases to the top, including re-selecting the active tab. Preserve the root filter/presentation. Every submenu retains its parent’s exact first visible item and pixel offset; Android Back and the in-menu back button use the same return path for albums, artists, album artists, folders, playlists, queues, and search results. Do not attach a restored list state to temporary empty results while its query loads. Back dismisses the keyboard or topmost menu/dialog first, then queue overlay or expanded player as applicable; selection is never cleared by Back; browsing Back returns from search/details, then retraces tab visits. Tab-history navigation defaults on; exit confirmation defaults off. Disabling tab history does not disable detail/search Back. Closing the app screen leaves playback running. Activity recreation restores navigation; a fresh launch starts at Home if configured, otherwise the first tab. Re-selecting the current tab adds no history.

Use the Apple Music Bottom Navbar&Miniplayer reference: three separated floating pills with aligned outer edges and consistent gaps. The full-width mini-player sits above a flexible configurable-tab pill at left and an unlabeled Search/More pill at right. Respect system insets; allow overflow scrolling only within the tab pill. Lists draw behind the floating controls, with scroll-content padding that lets the final items clear them; keep the artwork and title inset together inside the mini-player’s curved leading edge. The mini-player appears when an active queue has a current song, including while paused. Icons start at 24 dp with at least 48 dp touch targets. Artwork sits left, title and secondary metadata centrally, and black, filled Apple Music-like previous/play-pause/next icons right on a suitably light mini-player surface. Secondary metadata defaults to album artist with artist fallback; artist-first is selectable. Long titles pause, scroll left, and fade at the edges; reduced motion disables automatic scrolling.

Tapping the mini-player body or swiping upward expands it; transport buttons do not. One expansion progress drives the player surface, the downward exit of the main tabs, and appearance of the player's floating shelf. Full player order is artwork, seek bar/times, metadata, compact actions, transport, and Localica’s destination shelf; omit volume controls. Upward swipes across the player open Queue from that shelf; one gesture changes one surface level. The complete Queue button, including its label, opens the same destination. Swipe down or Back collapses the player without losing underlying query, filter, route, or scroll position. Song taps that start playback expand by default, with a stay-browsing setting; append/add-next do not expand.

Keep the established light browsing/settings surfaces regardless of Android appearance while the layout is being refined. Provide the matching inherited text/icon color; configurable dark styling belongs to later theme work. Use simple surfaces for the initial implementation. Shared visual-material boundaries will later provide floating translucency and the expanded player's more opaque, artwork-tinted blurred gradient. Preserve older-device and reduced-motion fallbacks.

## Browsing, search, and selection

Use shared templates for catalogs, collection contents, search, and expanded Home collections. Support list and compact/standard/large gallery presentation, retained by list type with optional collection overrides. Library All contains collections only, defaulting to Albums, Album Artists, and Playlists. Settings → Library and tags → Library filters controls which collection types All includes (also Artists/Folders), visible filters, and their order. Holding any filter opens the shared popup with a shortcut to that settings page. Keep at least one filter visible; an empty All selection is allowed. If the active filter is hidden, use the first remaining filter. Global search keeps every item type available. Use the visible labels Album Artist and Album Artists consistently; retain existing internal preference keys.

Show embedded covers in list/gallery modes. Gallery cards have no overlaid menu buttons; long-press opens collection actions. Keep galleries at full width and overlay a narrow scrollbar at the edge. Artist and album-artist images use representative album artwork; playlists initially use artwork from their contents. Choose covers deterministically, load appropriately sized images on demand through a bounded cancellable cache, and invalidate on rescan. No scraping. Keep a cover-provider boundary for later custom playlist images; assignment is a later capability.

The Library root has no top search field; global library search opens only from the bottom Search icon. Other lists may show scoped quick search, controlled by List View Options, with distinct Sort and View controls. Search is a route, never a main tab. Focus the field and show the keyboard immediately when opening Search; a direct field tap focuses it normally. Store completed searches as distinct routes, retaining query, scope, filters, sort, results position, and route identity. Submit/IME Search or opening a result completes the current query; typing edits one draft, not one history entry per keystroke. Starting another query preserves the previous completed route. Back retraces searches in order without automatically reopening the keyboard on restored results. Global results include songs and collections with type filters; scoped results match items eligible in the originating view. Retain query, scope, order, and position when opening results/player. A new queue from mixed results contains only the displayed song occurrences, in displayed order.

Lists of at least 40 items that overflow the viewport have an overlaid scrollbar with a narrow edge drag area and an accessibility scroll action. While dragging only, title sorting previews the current letter and year sorting previews the current year. Programmatic scrolling eases through the list; reduced motion skips animation.

Every applicable list has an unobtrusive sort icon. Changing sort normally changes the list-type default: album catalogs and album contents are distinct types, so changing one album's song sort changes all album contents. A checkbox can override the default for a specific collection; reset restores inheritance. Navigation/search text is independent of persistent sort preferences. Defaults: catalogs/search title ascending; album contents disc then track; playlists/queues saved order; recent collections newest; frequent collections most-played; pins manual. Offer meaningful fields only. Display sorting never silently rewrites saved playlist/queue order.

Hide bulk controls normally. Use rounded outlines for selected songs and collections instead of checkboxes; show a dashed outline when only some songs in a collection are selected. Long-pressing a song selects it and reveals a reusable bottom action panel above the mini-player, keeping tabs available. Collection long-press opens an icon-led action menu including Select all songs; normal collection taps navigate. Selection stores source occurrence identities and selection order across routes, tabs, queries, sorting, rotation, and backgrounding. Back does not clear it. A fresh session starts empty.

Select-all appends in configured collection order; repeated selection of the same source occurrence does not duplicate it. Deselect/reselect appends at the end. Preserve duplicates from distinct sources/occurrences for queues/playlists; deduplicate physical files for editing. Provide All, Range, count, actions, and explicit close. Only close or successful action exits; canceled/failed actions and empty selection retain the mode. Panel collapse does not clear. Selection actions wrap to available width and scroll within a bounded panel on short screens; wide landscape queues combine their header controls to keep songs reachable. Remove-from-source requires all entries in one editable source. No selection-basket screen.

In manual, unfiltered queue/playlist order, holding a song and continuing to drag moves that occurrence or the selected occurrences when all belong to this source. Preserve source-list order even for nonconsecutive selections. The top dragged card tracks the finger directly without a spring delay; animate the initial lift and landing independently. Lower cards follow the display frame clock with a short, bounded trailing offset. Apply pointer and follower positions during placement without recomposing or measuring card contents. Render the entire stack in the app foreground above selection actions and outside list clipping; the cancel circle remains above the stack. Lift visible selected rows into a shared-shadow stack: first selected song on top, slightly offset trailing cards, and a count badge for multiple songs. Keep a one-song insertion gap, animate surrounding rows, scroll near viewport edges, and smoothly unpack on release. A foreground floating X circle sits above the stack. Entering its hit area turns it red and emits a firm single haptic; leaving emits a lighter tick, respecting system haptic settings. Releasing inside cancels. Cancel/failure restores the list; reordering retains selection and playback cursors. Sorted/filtered views and selections spanning sources cannot reorder. The single-song playlist chooser emphasizes playlists already containing the song with an accessible label; duplicate additions remain permitted.

## Shared controls and reference layouts

Use the screenshots in the supplied localica-reference folder as structural references, including the newer Apple Music navigation/player and Musicolet scanner examples. Central corner tokens: 8 dp small art, 16 dp cards/inputs, 20 dp menus, 24 dp large art, 28 dp panels. Use pills for compact shelves/chips and circles for transport. Each control has a unified icon/label/surface target of at least 48 dp. Reuse icon-led action menus, bottom panels with consistent headers/dismissal/confirmation, and full-page settings/editors. Avoid redundant and placeholder actions.

Keep a persistent gesture owner and artwork/title/transport elements throughout expansion; interpolate measured geometry and corner masks. Track the finger directly, then settle by distance and velocity, including slow drags, reversals, interruptions and cancellation. Defaults: about 400 ms surfaces, 300 ms menus, 350 ms lyrics, with faster and reduced-motion preferences. Child lyrics/scrolling/slider/reorder/carousel gestures retain ownership even at an edge. Short-screen scrolling takes precedence. Anchored popups animate from the invoking control; materials remain replaceable for glass.

Customize compact player actions by long-pressing their empty center or through Settings: add/remove/reorder, centered alignment, one/two rows, smaller visual icons with full targets. Offer functional information/edit/art/lyrics/playlist/pin/collection-link/shuffle/repeat/count/ten-second-seek/More actions only. Default left Info/Add to playlist, right Repeat/Shuffle. Keep the center available for customization and the destination shelf extensible.

Queue has a prominent selector, position/count/duration, playlist saving, occurrence menus and drag ordering. Selecting a queue browses it without changing playback by default; selecting one of its song occurrences activates it. Optional switching preferences can restore paused, resume immediately, or retain playback state. A fading Current song button appears when the playing occurrence is offscreen and eases it to the top or the closest possible position. During dragging or queue selection it slides down and fades out, leaving the bottom area to selection actions. New queue names describe their originating list, such as Album ◀ Artist or All Songs ◀ Artist; mixed-artist lists omit the artist suffix. Names remain editable. Persist activation recency separately from manual order. Deleting the active queue always restores the most recently activated remaining queue’s saved occurrence and position, autoplaying only if the deleted queue was playing. Deleting/editing an inactive queue does not interrupt playback. Disable drag ordering while filtered or otherwise sorted. Queue management supports rename/reorder/delete/remove others. Information includes artwork, file/location, known technical information, metadata links, and Edit tags. Tag editor puts artwork first and shares the recent-image/file chooser, retaining checked fields, review, verification and recovery. Settings uses icon-led category rows, summaries, chevrons, and dedicated titled subpages with Back; search appears only at the root. Group existing settings into Interface, Playback and queues, Library and tags, and About/licenses. Do not show empty categories for unavailable features. List gear buttons open an English List View Options subpage following the reference: view mode, gallery size, extra information with a radio-choice dialog, applicable grouping controls, and scoped quick-search preference; preserve per-type defaults and collection overrides.

Extend metadata reading/editing to composer and lyricist, and editing to genre. Read file modification dates without editing them. Carry metadata through indexed records, search, snapshots, information and verified writes. Sort/display applicable name/count/duration/year/modified/added/play-count/artist/album-artist/composer/genre fields. Date added and Date modified initially sort newest first; Most played initially sorts highest first. Users can reverse each order. Collection summaries use earliest known year, latest modification, and summed known duration marked if partial; unknown sorts last with stable ties. Migrate metadata and queue ordering nondestructively; preserve IDs/cursors/grants/preferences and treat absent snapshot fields as unknown.

## Scanner and folders

More → Scan music opens a rounded scanner dialog with Scan now, live progress, total library songs, Folders to scan, and Metadata read failures. Its gear opens scanner configuration under Library and tags. Settings contains configuration only, never a manual scan action. The scanner and its settings link to the same dedicated folders page with folder names/paths, remove controls, and Add folder. Metadata failures belong in their scanner list, not a persistent queue warning.

Scanning can continue after leaving the popup/app. Once started, offer Continue in background and cancellation; own the operation outside the Activity with visible progress and completion/failure state. Explain genuinely missing permissions/restrictions and provide the closest app-specific Android settings destination. Android does not permit arbitrary scrolling of another app’s settings: prefer a direct Localica settings page over an unfiltered application list. Preserve folder grants, serialize scans with tag edits, and retain a usable index on cancellation or failure. Scanner configuration covers subfolders, minimum duration, excluded relative paths per folder, and optional scanning on launch; adding a folder does not start scanning automatically.

## Continuous playback

Keep one player with the active queue timeline; do not stop/reprepare during natural advancement. Preload about five seconds of the actual next track under repeat/shuffle, on by default with a Preload next track setting. Use Media3 playlist preloading and ordinary loading priorities; do not cache the whole queue or overlap/fade songs. Honor encoder delay/padding, preserve intentional silence, and verify audio boundaries. Codec/device/format changes can limit gaplessness. PCM, FLAC, LAME/Xing MP3 and M4A with complete sample timing have decoded-boundary fixtures. Remaining padding tails affect some FFmpeg M4A files with shortened final sample durations ([#28](https://github.com/anastaci1a/localica/issues/28)) and MP3 playback with the legacy Android 8 decoder ([#30](https://github.com/anastaci1a/localica/issues/30)).

Invalidate obsolete prepared sources on queue/source changes. Preparation errors must leave current playback intact. Before writing a file represented in the active queue, capture current entry/cursor/playing state and release prepared sources including duplicate occurrences; rebuild/restore after verified completion or recovery. Edits outside the active queue do not interrupt playback. Crossfades are a separate later option.

## Artwork and lyrics

Offer two persisted artwork layouts, defaulting to full-background artwork. Scale artwork uniformly to screen width without cropping its sides; keep its established vertical center. Following the Apple Music player reference, progressively soften detail into broad, curved color regions that preserve the cover’s left/right colors, extending smoothly above and below the image. Use a feathered transparency mask over this continuation, with gentle lower shading for controls; avoid a straight fade to one averaged color. Generate the small filtered texture when artwork changes, off the UI thread, and reuse it during gestures. The alternative uses a floating cover over an artwork-colored matte/acrylic gradient with subtle texture. Keep the floating Queue shelf visually independent of this background. Both modes animate into the same lyrics layout when artwork is tapped: lyrics blend into the upper screen without a panel boundary or Lyrics heading; transport/metadata ease lower and optional extra actions fade/slide away. Retain transport access. Use [Better Lyrics](https://github.com/better-lyrics/better-lyrics) as strong visual inspiration for readable active-line emphasis and fluid following. Fade text at the top and bottom viewport edges as lines enter and leave. Respect reduced motion and keep accessibility focus stable.

Synced lyrics follow the real playback position, including seeks and queue changes. Highlight the current line and allow tapping a timed line to seek. Manual scrolling temporarily releases automatic following; provide a return-to-current-line action. Touch exploration starts with following disabled to keep reading focus stable. Unsynced lyrics use ordinary scrolling without invented timestamps. Standard LRC supports line timing; richer word timing may be used only when present in a supported source. The initial reader displays line timing, including millisecond-based ID3 SYLT; frame-count SYLT timing remains unsupported. Show honest empty/error states and expose available source choices from the [local source policy](product.md#lyrics).

Holding artwork opens an anchored popup by default with **View artwork**, **Change artwork**, and **Configure tap action**. View artwork opens a dedicated viewer with pinch zoom/pan and an accessible reset; Back returns to the same player state. Change artwork opens the shared image chooser and edits the current song only through the verified tag-write path. The chooser offers quick recent-image thumbnails and a full files/gallery picker, handling partial access and cancellation. Bulk artwork editing remains an explicit selection action.

Configure tap and hold independently in Settings: toggle lyrics, show the artwork menu, view artwork, change artwork, or do nothing. The default menu provides the tap shortcut; Settings and accessible player actions remain available even when gestures are remapped. Back closes menus/pickers/viewer first, then the visible lyrics panel, then collapses the player. Preserve the underlying browse state and prevent artwork gestures from unintentionally expanding/collapsing the player.

## Personalized Home and statistics

Local date and listening statistics support catalog sorting and the Most played song tab. Configurable Home carousels remain a later target, before optional Last.fm integration; they do not block starting liquid-glass work on the established layouts.

Default carousels are pinned mixed items, frequently played mixed items, recently added albums, and recently added songs. Titles with chevrons open the full collection. Allow add/remove/reorder, source, sorting, presentation, and preview-count customization (ten items initially). Carousel choices override view-type defaults. Pins support songs/albums/playlists with manual order.

Record the first successful indexing date per song; rescans retain it. Albums, Artists, Album Artists, and Folders use the earliest added date and latest known file modification date among their current distinct songs. Existing tracks receive the migration date. Removing a song from the index and later adding it again starts a new added date. Playlists use their creation/import date and the time of their last rename or membership/order edit; metadata refreshes and no-op edits do not change that date. Old playlists initially use their saved creation date for both. Do not invent previous history.

A qualified play requires actual listening for 30 seconds or half the track duration, whichever is shorter, once per playback traversal. Paused time and seek jumps do not count. Albums, Artists, Album Artists, and Folders sum their current distinct songs’ counts; playlist counts attribute qualified songs to the playlist from which their queue entries originated, including after switching queues. Statistics are local and all-time, retained independently of folders and saved queues. Old queues have unknown playlist origin and do not invent one. Pauses resume the current traversal; automatic repeats, switching occurrences, and explicit restarts at zero begin a new traversal. Unqualified partial listening is held only while the service lives; saved qualified plays survive restarts. The Most played tab initially shows qualified songs, highest count first. Empty collections have honest empty states.

## Delivery and verification

Use typed restorable destinations, typed media references, per-type preferences/collection overrides, occurrence-aware queue commands with optional source attribution, shared expansion state, and replaceable material components. Preserve existing libraries, playlists, queues, and listening preferences.

Verify Back precedence/settings, tab overflow, player gestures and transport, restored queries/positions, session-wide selection ordering/cancellation/accessibility, sorting inheritance, duplicate occurrences, complete queue snapshots, artwork reuse/invalidation, pins/dates/play counts, migrations, small/landscape windows, large fonts, reduced motion, and Android 8/16. Editing and lyrics checkpoints also cover safe tag writes and partial failures, single-song artwork scope, recent-image access/cancellation, zoom and gesture remapping, embedded/sidecar precedence, malformed lyrics, and synchronization after seeking or changing songs. Each milestone produces a verified development APK.
