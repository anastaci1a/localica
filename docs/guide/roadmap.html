Roadmap

Status: proposed work. These outcomes require implementation or verification. They do not establish released behavior.

Current checkpoint

Released v0.30.0 delivered gesture ownership, queue reuse and the shared glass controls. The next checkpoint is physical-device confidence in responsiveness and stability, judged against release gates.

Improve startup and dense-library responsiveness (#406).

Investigate the open stability reports: repeated pinching at Smallest (#474) and Legacy renderer observer failures during navigation (#475).

Resolve the post-release defects #491 to #496 and the test-reliability reports #498 and #499. Clean up inert CI workflows (#497).

Require reproducible optical comparisons before changing glass defaults.

Review the material and control contracts on representative physical devices: depth response, interrupted gestures, large collections and material handoffs.

Validate rendering performance, especially queue scrolling and player expansion. Emulator measurements do not establish physical-device frame rates. Reproduce intermittent manual-scroll jitter.

Validate selection appearance and reversal on physical devices, preserving accessibility and reduced motion.

Verify AAC track boundaries on physical output. Decoded sample equality does not establish seamless speaker playback.

Deferred capabilities

These do not gate visual work. Issues own detailed acceptance criteria.

Capability Scope / dependency
Metadata parsing Configurable title rules and separate Artist/Album Artist splitting, previews/exceptions, preserving raw tags; #74.
Personalized Home Extend current typed/manual pins into configurable carousels: pinned mixed items, frequently played mixed items, recently added albums/songs. Default ten previews; customize sources/order/sort/presentation/count, overriding type defaults. Chevron titles open complete collections.
Last.fm Off by default; explicit connection/scrobbling, durable pending submissions, disconnect/status controls, reviewed/resettable track/artist matching. No automatic upload of past history or rewriting local tags; local counts remain independent. Verify protocol eligibility separately from local counting (track >30s, half duration or four minutes listened). Retain actual listening duration/start timestamps.
Lyric lookup Opt-in lookup when local lyrics are absent, investigating Better Lyrics’ backend and protected .lrc sidecar saving; #187.
Lyric editor Manual .lrc editing and playback-assisted line timestamp taps, with correction/undo, preview and recoverable explicit save/export.
Visualizers Player button for Poweramp-compatible presets. Review specification: MilkDrop v1/v2, shaders/textures, extensions/packaging, licensing and device performance.
Other candidates Crossfades, more shuffle modes, sleep timer, equalizer, system/light/dark themes, reduced transparency with solid fallback, multilingual UI, smart playlists, bookmarks/favorites, widgets, Android Auto and backup/export. Prioritize explicitly before implementation.
