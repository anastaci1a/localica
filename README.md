# localica

An Android player for music stored on your device. Choose a folder, browse its songs and subfolders, and play one track at a time.

Two tabs. Play/pause and seeking. Background playback. No account or internet access.

<img src="docs/images/player.png" width="250" alt="Player with track details, progress, and playback control"> <img src="docs/images/library.png" width="250" alt="Library listing audio scanned from a folder and its subfolders">

Screenshots show the app with generated sample tones.

Requires **Android 8+**. Supports MP3 and AAC/M4A, plus WAV, FLAC, Ogg, and Opus where the device provides a decoder. Playback stops when the track ends.
